# 28 — Round 9: pace

Status: build, 2026-10-24. The owner said more than once that animations were too fast. A user can change one action at a time, but not the pace of a whole video.

| # | Item |
|---|---|
| P1 | Done. **Pace**: Scene › Video › Slower (all times 25 % longer) or Faster (20 % shorter), in one undo step; also in the command palette and `anivo pace <scene> <factor> [--steps …]`. Delays, durations, staggers, holds, and transitions change; flow particles move slower or faster by the same amount. Cues in a voiceover do not change. |
| P2 | Done. **Pace of one step**: Slower and Faster in the step settings change only that step. |
